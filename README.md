# Inventory_Sales_Dashboard-PowerBI

**Introduction**

The Inventory Sales Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze sales performance, customer insights, sales representative performance, purchase orders, and warehouse operations. It helps businesses monitor key performance indicators, understand sales trends, and analyze inventory and purchasing activities for better data-driven decision-making.

**Key Metrics**

Total Sales : Total sales generated across the available sales data

Total Customers : Count of customers available in the dataset

Active Customers : Number of currently active customers

Total Sales Reps : Total number of sales representatives

Total Purchase Orders : Number of purchase orders created

Total Ordered Quantity : Total quantity of items ordered

Total Received Quantity : Total quantity of items received

Active Warehouses : Number of active warehouses

Average Delay : Average delivery delay for sales/orders

Average Discount : Average discount applied to sales

Average Tax : Average tax applied to sales

**Visualizations**

Sales by Month – Displays monthly sales performance and trends

Top 10 Items by Quantity – Shows the items with the highest quantities sold

Average Discount by Category – Compares the average discount across product categories

Average Tax by Category – Analyzes average tax across different categories

Sales by Payment Method – Displays sales distribution across different payment methods

Production Line Sales – Compares sales performance across production lines

Top 10 Customers by Sales – Identifies customers contributing the highest sales

Customers by State – Shows customer distribution across different states

Sales Representative Performance – Analyzes sales performance by sales representative

Purchase Orders by Warehouse – Compares purchase order activity across warehouses

Ordered vs Received Quantity – Compares quantities ordered with quantities received

Purchase Orders by Month – Displays monthly purchase order trends

Top Organizations by Ordered Quantity – Shows organizations with the highest ordered quantities

**Cards**

Total Sales : Shows overall sales generated

Total Customers : Displays the total customer count

Active Customers : Indicates the number of active customers

Total Sales Reps : Shows the total number of sales representatives

Total Purchase Orders : Displays the number of purchase orders

Total Ordered Qty : Shows the total quantity ordered

Total Received Qty : Indicates the total quantity received

Active Warehouses : Shows the number of active warehouses

**Filters**

Interactive slicers and filters are used for dynamic exploration:

Period: Select different time periods for analyzing sales and inventory performance.

Customer State: Filter customer and sales information based on state.

Customer Status: Filter customers based on their current status.

Sales Representative: Analyze sales and performance for individual representatives.

Representative Type: Filter sales representatives based on their type.

**Usage**

Open the Dashboard: Launch the `.pbix` file in Power BI Desktop.

Explore the Visuals: Navigate through the Sales Overview, Customer Insights, and Inventory Operations pages to analyze business performance.

Apply Filters: Use the available slicers and filters to explore specific periods, states, customers, sales representatives, and other categories.

Interact Dynamically: Click on charts and data points to highlight related information and explore deeper insights across the dashboard.

Use Tooltips: Hover over visual elements to view additional information through the dedicated tooltip pages.

**Conclusion**

This Inventory Sales Dashboard provides an interactive analytical solution for understanding sales performance, customer behavior, sales representative performance, purchasing activity, and warehouse operations. It enables users to monitor important business metrics, identify trends, and gain meaningful insights from sales and inventory data.
